How to play

  • Move WASD / arrows, or drag anywhere on a phone
  • Aim with the mouse, or let auto-aim pick the nearest target
  • Dash Space or the DASH button: you can't be hit mid-dash
  • Level up by grabbing green shards, then pick an upgrade (1 2 3)
  • Every 5th level repairs 2 shields
  • Blades shoot and block enemy shots, but break after 5 hits
  • P pause · M mute all · N music on/off · F show FPS
